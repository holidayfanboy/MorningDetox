Morning Detox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Summary

Morning Detox is an alarm and phone-detox app. It collects anonymous usage analytics so we can see how often the app is opened, whether people keep using it, and which features get used — and improve it accordingly.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”.

What is collected

Only while “Share anonymous usage data” is turned on (it is on by default). Collection is handled by Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • A random app-instance identifier — generated on install, not tied to your name or any account, and reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data.
  • Device and app information — device model, operating-system version, app version, device language, and the country/region derived from your IP address. The IP address itself is not retained in analytics reports.
  • In-app activity — the app being opened and brought back to the foreground; which screens are viewed; alarms created, edited, deleted or enabled/disabled; detox sessions started and how they ended (completed or ended early); settings changed; and the number of apps you add to the “allowed apps” list.

What is NOT collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Photos, files, or anything else on your device.
  • Precise or GPS location.
  • The text of your alarm labels / memos.
  • Which specific apps you add to the “allowed apps” list (only how many).
  • Any advertising identifier — ad personalization is disabled.

Why we collect it

To understand aggregate usage — how often the app is opened, how many people return over time, and which features are adopted — so we can decide what to build and fix. Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are your consent (the in-app toggle) and our legitimate interest in improving the app.

Who processes it

Google LLC, as our analytics provider (Google Analytics for Firebase). Data may be processed on Google servers in the United States and other countries.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

How long it is kept

Event-level analytics data is retained for up to 14 months and then automatically deleted by Google. Aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.

Your choices

  • Turn it off: Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”. This stops all collection from your device.
  • Uninstall the app to stop collection entirely.
  • To ask what data is associated with your app-instance identifier, or to request its deletion, contact us at the address below.

Children

Morning Detox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be called out in the app’s release notes.
